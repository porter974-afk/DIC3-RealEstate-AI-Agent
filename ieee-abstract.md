# IEEE-Style Abstract — Real Estate Decision AI Agent

**Title: A Real Estate Decision AI Agent Integrating Transaction Records, Spatial Features, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence**

**Abstract—** Residential property valuation is a complex decision-making problem influenced by building characteristics, spatial heterogeneity, and changing market conditions. Although Taiwan's publicly available real estate transaction records provide valuable historical information, their practical use is constrained by data inconsistencies, spatial dependencies, and limited interpretability of predictive models. This study proposes a real estate decision-support agent that integrates transaction data, geographic information system (GIS) features, machine learning, and explainable artificial intelligence (XAI). The proposed framework incorporates data preprocessing, spatial feature engineering, and comparative price prediction using linear regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost. SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) will be employed to interpret model predictions, while a tool-grounded large language model agent will support natural-language queries, property comparisons, and evidence-based reporting. The evaluation will examine predictive accuracy using mean absolute error, root mean squared error, and mean absolute percentage error, alongside temporal and spatial validation, ablation studies, explanation stability, and agent reliability. The anticipated contribution is an integrated framework for transparent, traceable, and interpretable real estate decision support. The proposed approach aims to improve the practical usability of public transaction data without replacing professional appraisal or implying certainty about future market prices.

**Index Terms—** Real estate valuation, housing price prediction, geographic information systems, explainable artificial intelligence, SHAP, large language models, AI agents, decision support systems.
